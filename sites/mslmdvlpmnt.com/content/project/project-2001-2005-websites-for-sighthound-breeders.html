2001 – 2005 Websites for Sighthound Breeders

 Project Date: 08/02/2001

After the first web startup bubble burst in 2001, it became more difficult to find jobs or contracts as a freelancer.

I offered various greyhound breeders free websites in return for a share of the turnover. During this time I have created and maintained about a dozen websites.

I also experimented with selling online adverts for the first time during this time and started an online forum for pedigree dog breeders.
