Covid Neighbourhood Help Website

 Project Date: 19/03/2020

As the Covid pandemic raged and the first lockdowns were imposed, it became more difficult for certain groups of people, such as lonely pensioners and people with severe disabilities, to carry out daily tasks such as shopping. Various local initiatives popped up online to help with this.
This website collected all these initiatives and listed them by postcode.
