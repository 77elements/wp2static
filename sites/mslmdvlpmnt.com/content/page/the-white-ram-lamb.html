In a vibrant mega-metropolis of the year 2140, where technology and tradition have inseparably merged, an ordinary man pushes against the boundaries of his everyday life. Called by a mysterious symbol, he ventures into an unknown world that transforms him at breathtaking speed: from a simple citizen to a leader who redefines the foundations of his community. This gripping novel in the style of Muslim Futurism weaves science fiction elements with spiritual depths into an epic tale of awakening and transformation.

Every scene, every detail comes from an intense dream the author experienced, who saw the story more vividly than any film before his mind’s eye.

Immerse yourself in a future that’s approaching faster than you think, and experience a transformation that might call your own life into question.

About the story

The story is about an ordinary man who lives a humble life with his wife on the outskirts of a megacity in the future. He works as an android tester and has the same dull routine every day. Until a call from afar appears and takes him on an unexpected journey.

The story takes place in a totalitarian surveillance state, where the classic nation-state has been replaced by megacities and is subject to the intrusive government of the rich and powerful.

As the story unfolds, his wife and he undergo an unexpected transformation, find a new community, and discover their true calling.

About the Origin

This story appeared to the author in a dream in early 2025 and played out in it visually, like a movie. One of those dreams where you have every scene clearly in your head after waking up; something that rarely happens.

So he decided in early summer 2025 to write a book based on this story, even though he had never written a book in his life before. This is his debut work.

The book is produced entirely under his own direction and is also self-published. You won’t find it in regular bookstores.
