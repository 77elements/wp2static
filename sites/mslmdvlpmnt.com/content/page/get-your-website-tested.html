It is not easy to determine whether a website is “good” and how good it really is. Many factors play a role in this. And it is also not easy to find someone who can tell you that objectively. There are no automated tests for this. You need someone with experience in all disciplines of website building.

What’s being tested?

The website test covers 7 categories:

Goal fulfillment, Technical appropriateness, Visual design, Usability, Accessibility, Communication design, Ethics.

Each category is scored separately. The overall score is calculated equally from the scores of all 7 tests.
In the ethical test, only the last category is assessed.
