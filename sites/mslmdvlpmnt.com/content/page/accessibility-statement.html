Accessibility Statement

My Commitment to Accessibility

I am committed to ensuring digital accessibility for people with disabilities. I strive to continually improve the user experience for everyone and apply the relevant accessibility standards.

Conformance Status

My website aims to conform to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 and WCAG 2.2 at the AA level. These guidelines explain how to make web content more accessible for people with a wide array of disabilities, including visual, auditory, physical, speech, cognitive, language, learning, and neurological disabilities.

Measures to Support Accessibility

To ensure accessibility, I have taken the following measures:

  • Integrated accessibility into my web development processes
  • Completed accessibility training
  • Conducted regular accessibility audits and user testing
  • Used third-party tools and assistive technology for evaluation

Limitations and Alternatives

Despite my best efforts to ensure accessibility of my website, there may be some limitations. Below is a description of known limitations and potential solutions:

  • Some third-party content may not fully meet accessibility standards.
  • Certain older documents may not be fully accessible.
